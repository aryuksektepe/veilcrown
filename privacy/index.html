Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-20. Developer: Ali Rıza Yüksektepe. App: Veilcrown (bundle ID com.yuksektepe.veilcrown), iPhone and iPad.

Short version

Veilcrown does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not receive anything about you or your play.

What stays on your device

Your progress is stored in a save file inside the app on your device: gold, level, XP, items you own, settings such as language, stats such as games played and your Elo against the bots, badges, puzzle results, and your last finished game. This file is not sent to the developer.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Veilcrown also writes the same save to iCloud key-value storage (Apple's service) so your progress can follow you to your other devices. This data lives in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot read it. You can turn it off for Veilcrown in the Settings app under your name, iCloud, and delete it under Manage Account Storage. Apple's own privacy policy covers how iCloud works.

In-app purchases

Purchases are made and processed by Apple through the App Store. Veilcrown does not see your name, email address, payment method or Apple Account. The app only learns that a product was bought, so it can unlock the item. Restore purchases asks Apple which products your account owns.

What Veilcrown does not do

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is used.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking, from us or from third parties.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No chat and no user-generated content.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

The game works offline. The only network use is Apple's: the App Store for purchases and iCloud for the optional sync. The Privacy button in Settings opens this page in your browser.

Children

Veilcrown has no ads, no chat, no accounts and collects no data, so it can be used by children without data going anywhere. Purchases need the Apple Account holder, and parents can use Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time to control them. The game shows fantasy fight scenes between chess pieces, without blood.

Your rights and deleting data

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Deleting the app removes the local save. To remove the iCloud copy, use Manage Account Storage as described above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with data, this page will be updated first and the effective date will change.
